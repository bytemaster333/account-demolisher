Account Demolisher closes a Stellar account and sends everything it holds to an address
you choose. Closing an account releases the XLM that Stellar keeps locked in it, which is
the part a plain "send all my funds" transfer leaves behind.

You keep control the whole way. The app never sees your secret key, it shows you every
step before you sign, and nothing is submitted until you confirm.

## What gets cleaned up

Closing an account means clearing everything attached to it first. The app does that for
you in one reviewed plan:

| Attached to your account                         | What happens                            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Tokens other than XLM                            | Swapped to XLM, then sent with the rest |
| Open buy and sell offers                         | Cancelled                               |
| Trustlines                                       | Emptied, then removed                   |
| Saved data entries                               | Deleted                                 |
| Extra signers and raised thresholds              | Cleared and reset                       |
| Pending payments set aside for you               | Collected into your balance             |
| Deposits your account locked for its own entries | Released back to you                    |
| Blend, Aquarius, Soroswap, and FxDAO positions   | Withdrawn or repaid before the close    |
| Token spending permissions                       | Set to zero                             |

## Where the funds can go

Any Stellar address that already exists on the network. Two cases behave differently:

- **A wallet you control.** The account merges straight into it.
  See [Close an account](/docs/users/close-an-account).
- **An exchange deposit address.** Exchanges credit payments, not account merges, so the
  app routes the funds through a short-lived helper account that carries your deposit memo.
  See [Send the funds to an exchange](/docs/users/send-to-an-exchange).
