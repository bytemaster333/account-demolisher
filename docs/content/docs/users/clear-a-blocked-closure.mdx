---
title: Clear a blocked closure
description: What to do about anything the app asks you to fix or acknowledge before you can sign.
icon: TriangleAlert
---

The app checks the account before it lets you sign, and stops for two different reasons.
**Fix issues** means something needs an action from you. **Warnings** means something needs
reading, not fixing.

A few conditions stop a close entirely. Those are on this page too.

## Things you have to fix

### A balance that cannot be converted to XLM

The close turns every non-XLM balance into XLM. When no market path exists for one, the
account cannot close while it still holds it, and you get a **Fix issues** step.

Choose per asset:

- **Send to your destination.** Offered only when your destination already accepts that
  token.
- **Return to issuer.** Sends it back to the address that created it. This is permanent and
  you almost certainly get nothing back.

You can also sell or move the token in another app first, then select **Re-check and
continue** to rebuild the plan.

Until you choose something for each one, that balance keeps blocking the close.

## Things you only have to read

These appear in the **Warnings** step. None of them stop the close. Tick each box, then
continue.

### A token that looks suspicious

Three checks run over the account's tokens and flag anything matching a known scam
pattern:

| Flag                  | What tripped it                                                               |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Impersonation**     | Uses a well-known asset's symbol but a different issuer than the official one |
| **Look-alike**        | The symbol is one or two characters away from a well-known one                |
| **Hidden characters** | The symbol contains characters outside A to Z and 0 to 9                      |
| **Unrecognized**      | The token is not on the list of known ones                                    |

The check is not proof. A real asset can trip it. It is flagged so you can confirm you
recognize what you held before you continue.

### The scan could not finish

When a scan does not complete, the app says so rather than proceeding as though the
account were fully understood. You see which one fell short: token permissions, DeFi
positions, or the optional standalone-token scan.

If you have used Blend, Aquarius, Soroswap, or FxDAO, check those apps before closing so
nothing is left behind.

### A large balance

An account holding more than 1000 XLM gets its own acknowledgement. Check the amount and
the destination once more before you continue.

### Things handled for you

Pending payments waiting for the account, and deposits the account locked for its own
entries, are collected and released during the close. You keep the value and there is
nothing to do.

## Things that stop the close completely

### This account can't be closed

The account has the `auth_immutable` flag set. Stellar forbids merging an account with
that flag, and the flag is permanent, so no tool can close it.

Your funds are safe and the account works normally. You can keep using it, or move the
funds out to another account yourself.

### This account is paying deposits for other accounts

The account is sponsoring entries that belong to other accounts. Stellar does not allow an
account to close while it does that.

The owner of those entries has to remove them, using another tool such as the Stellar
Laboratory. Once they are gone, come back and try again.

Entries the account sponsors for _itself_ are different. Those are released during the
close automatically and do not block anything.

### A Blend position you have to deal with first

Two Blend cases need action before the account can close: a loan you cannot currently
repay, and a backstop deposit with its 17-day queue. Both are covered in
[Close an account that holds DeFi](/docs/users/close-with-defi).

## Custom tokens that would be left behind

A SEP-41 token held in its own contract does not block the close, and it stays on the
deleted account unless you move it. The Warnings step lists these under **Standalone
tokens** so you can tick the ones to sweep.

An exchange destination cannot receive them at all. Move any you want to keep to a
personal wallet first.
