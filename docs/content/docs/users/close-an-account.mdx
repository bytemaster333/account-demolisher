Closing an account clears everything attached to it, converts what has a market to XLM, sends
the whole balance to an address you choose, and deletes the account from the ledger. The XLM
Stellar kept locked as reserve is part of what you receive.

A close cannot be undone, so the app puts several deliberate checks in front of the final
confirmation.

## Before you start

- Connect the account you want to close. See
  [Connect your wallet](/docs/users/connect-your-wallet).
- Have the destination address ready. It must already exist on the network, because a close
  merges into it.
- Sending to an exchange? Read [Send the funds to an exchange](/docs/users/send-to-an-exchange)
  first, because the deposit memo decides whether the exchange credits you.

## The steps

Four steps always appear: **Connect**, **Configure**, **Review** and **Close**. **Fix issues**
and **Warnings** appear when the plan has something for you to resolve or read, and
**Signatures** appears for a shared account.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Configure

Answer **Where should the funds go?**

**Destination address.** Paste the `G` address that receives everything. The app checks the
format, refuses your own account (the network rejects a self-merge), and reads the destination
from the network. A destination that does not exist yet blocks the close until you fund it.

**Memo.** Pick a type (Text, ID, Hash or Return) and paste the value. A personal wallet needs
none. On a direct close the memo rides on the transaction that closes the account. A destination
that requires a memo, a known exchange or an account flagged under SEP-29, keeps **Continue**
disabled until you enter a well-formed one.

Select **Continue, preview the plan**. The app reads the account, discovers its DeFi positions,
token allowances and standalone tokens, builds the plan and simulates every Soroban step against
the live network. Nothing is signed yet.

</Step>

<Step>

### Fix issues, when something keeps the close from starting

This step lists everything that stops the close, and each item that needs a choice gets one: a
balance with no market to XLM, a sale over the 2% price-impact limit, a token swap that was
refused, or Blend backstop rewards that cannot be claimed today. Make your choices, then select
**Re-check and continue**, which rebuilds the plan with them.

See [Clear a blocked closure](/docs/users/clear-a-blocked-closure).

</Step>

<Step>

### Warnings, when there is something to read

This step collects what you need to know but nothing you have to fix: a token that looks like a
copy of a known one, a scan that did not finish, a close that moves over 1000 XLM or holdings it
cannot price, payments the close collects for you, and notes about the plan.

Here you also see **Pending payments this close collects**, which lists every claimable balance
the account can claim now. A box starts ticked when the plan collects that balance. Untick one to
leave it behind. A balance the close cannot collect (no trustline it can open, an unauthorized
trustline, a full trustline, or no XLM for a new trustline's reserve) starts unticked, and ticking
it does not collect it: it stays under **Some pending payments will be left behind** with the
reason. A balance the account sponsors itself has no box and stays in the claim list, because
leaving it would keep the account a sponsor and block the merge. A change applies when you rebuild
the plan. On a run that only prepares the close, nothing is collected yet, so every box starts
unticked.

On a shared account this list does not appear. The close collects every pending payment it can,
and you cannot leave one out.

**Balances this close sells for XLM** lists every token balance the close sells at the market
price. For each one you choose **Sell for XLM** (the default), **Send it as-is to your
destination**, or **Return to issuer (keep nothing)**. Sending as-is is offered only when the
destination has an authorized trustline for that token with room for the whole balance, and never
on an exchange close, because the payment would arrive without your memo. A change applies when you
rebuild the plan. Right before the close transaction is signed, the app reads the destination again
for every balance you send as-is, counting the pending payments the same transaction collects into
it, and stops the close unsigned when the destination can no longer take the whole amount. On a
shared account the collected transaction gets the same check before it is submitted.

Tick each acknowledgement, then select **Continue to review**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Review

The heading reads **Review before closing**. At the top:

| Figure                          | Meaning                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Destination receives**        | What arrives at the destination                                                                                   |
| **Network fees**                | An estimate and the most the close can pay, shown as "about ... XLM, at most ... XLM". None of it goes to the app |
| **Reserve released (included)** | The XLM the network kept locked in the account, already part of the amount above                                  |

A step that cannot be built until the steps it depends on land, such as a withdrawal behind a
repay, has no fee yet. The fee figure then reads "about ... XLM plus N steps priced when they are built,
at most ... XLM": the estimate leaves those steps out, and the most counts each at the highest fee
the app signs for that kind of step. Each step in the plan shows its own fee, as "fee about ...
XLM, up to ... XLM", or as "fee up to ... XLM" for a step that is not built yet.

Below the figures is the plan, grouped by phase in the order it runs, such as **Token permissions**,
**Repay loans**, **Withdraw from apps**, **Collect rewards**, **Leftover tokens**, **Close the
account** and, for a close to an exchange or another address that requires a memo, **Deliver to
destination**. A group appears only when the plan has steps in it, and a Blend backstop or a
classic unwind ahead of a loan repayment adds its own group. The last two carry an
**Irreversible** badge.

Check the address under **SENDING EVERYTHING TO**, then select **Continue to final
confirmation**. The button stays disabled while anything in the review still blocks the close.

</Step>

<Step>

### Confirm

A dialog asks you to type the **last four characters** of the destination. They are underlined
in the address above the input. A four-second timer runs at the same time, and **Close account**
stays locked until the timer finishes and the characters match. This catches a wrong paste or a
swapped clipboard.

</Step>

<Step>

### Close

The app signs and submits each transaction in order. Approve each prompt in your wallet as it
appears.

Every Soroban step is its own transaction, because Stellar does not combine Soroban and classic
operations in one transaction. The classic close is one transaction for most accounts. It takes
more in these cases, each adding a signature:

- It holds over 100 operations.
- The account has a classic liquidity pool share. It is withdrawn in a transaction of its own
  first.
- A claimable balance in a token that is then sold on the market, or that needs a trustline the
  close opens, is claimed in a transaction of its own first, so the sale is sized from what
  actually landed.
- Two conversions trade through the same market. Each is quoted against the market as it is, so
  they run in separate transactions, and the later one is quoted again once the earlier one
  landed.

Right before each Soroban signature the progress line shows the network fee that signature allows.

<Callout type="warn">
  Keep the tab open until it finishes. Closing the tab stops the close partway. Steps that already
  landed stay on chain, and [When a step fails](/docs/users/when-a-step-fails) covers how to finish.
</Callout>

</Step>

</Steps>

## When it finishes

The heading reads **Account closed** with the amount sent and the destination. You get:

- A **PROOF** link to the merge transaction on a block explorer.
- **Copy a receipt**, which copies a text record of the closure with a link for every
  transaction it ran. Where the browser does not allow copying, the receipt text is shown on the
  page instead.
- **Download .txt**, which saves the same receipt as a text file.

Keep the receipt. A closed account cannot be looked up by its address afterwards.

## What it costs

The app is free and takes no cut. You pay the Stellar network fees of the transactions it
submits, and Review shows their estimate and maximum before you sign.

The classic transactions bid from what recent ledgers charged, and raise the bid when the network
rejects it as too low, up to 0.1 XLM per operation. On an exchange close, the forward to the
exchange starts at the same bid and goes up to 0.2 XLM in total. Each Soroban step bids a 0.01 XLM
inclusion fee plus the resource fee its simulation priced, and the app refuses to sign a step whose
fee is over the cap for its kind of step. For an exit or a conversion, Review marks such a step as
failed with the fee it would cost, and the close cannot be confirmed with that step in it. One
cause is a step that would first restore a token's archived contract code from the ledger
archive: the ones measured on testnet cost 3.7 to 17.4 XLM, and the app refuses them instead of
paying.

## If a step fails

Steps that landed stay on chain. Whether the account is still open depends on how far the close
got. [When a step fails](/docs/users/when-a-step-fails) explains each case and what **Retry** and
**Start over** do.
