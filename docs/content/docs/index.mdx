Account Demolisher closes a Stellar account from start to finish. It reads the account, unwinds
everything attached to it, sells what has a market for XLM, and merges the account into a
destination you pick. Every transaction is signed by your own wallet or key.

## What it closes

Classic Stellar entries, handled in one batched transaction (or a few, for a large account):

- Open DEX offers
- Trustlines, both credit trustlines and liquidity pool share trustlines
- Data entries
- Non-master signers and raised thresholds
- Claimable balances the account can claim
- Reserves the account holds for its own claimable balances

Soroban DeFi positions, each unwound in its own transaction before the classic close:

- Blend: repay loans, withdraw collateral and supply, claim emissions
- Blend backstop: claim emissions, queue the withdrawal, withdraw it after the unlock
- Aquarius: claim gauge and pool rewards, withdraw pool shares and concentrated price ranges
- Soroswap: withdraw the LP position
- SEP-41 token allowances: set the approval to zero
- Standalone SEP-41 tokens you select: send to the destination or convert to XLM

FxDAO is not on the list: the protocol shut down on chain, so there are no FxDAO positions left to
unwind. See [Close an account that holds DeFi](/docs/users/close-with-defi#fxdao).
